Campaign Editor

My ships

    Every ship is one of three classes: destroyer, battleship or carrier. The class sets toughness and behaviour; you design the size and shape.

    Use in the game

    Press Save. Ships with “mix into random maps” ticked show up on their own in generated missions (campaign and skirmish). In the Campaign Editor you can also add a specific ship, or a “random custom ship”, to any mission. On another device use Copy phone link and open it once there.

    Design (side view, bow points right)

    Drag the white dots to reshape the hull. Double-click a hull edge to add a point, double-click a point to remove it. Click a part to select and move it; AA guns sit on whatever surface is beneath them. The blue line is the waterline.
